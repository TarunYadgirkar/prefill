Expendite Fellowship Application (Cohort I, 2026)

Expendite Fellowship is a selective fellowship for early-stage founders outside major startup hubs. Focus is on execution clarity, decision-making under constraint, and producing real applied work.
FORMAT
Duration: 8 weeks Commitment: 5–10 hours per week Mode: Remote, discussion-based Work: Reading, debate, writing, document creation
Details for application here: www.expendite.org

Application may take 30-60mins. Please fill all information candidly.

Before you apply:
- This application is used to understand fit, not to judge polish or English quality.
- There is no payment required to apply.
- Accepted applicants will receive details before any payment is requested.
- This fellowship is process-heavy and requires sustained effort.
