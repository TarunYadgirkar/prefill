Exceptional companies are built by exceptional people.


Pear Prime is a highly curated network for high-potential CS students, new graduates, and early-career engineers. We connect members with founders and technical leaders at some of the most ambitious companies in technology—from startups in their earliest days to frontier AI labs.


More than a career program, Prime is a lifelong community of ambitious builders, designed to support members as they grow into founding engineers, technical leaders, and founders.
